How it works

1 · forecast normal demand for today from past data only · 2 · compare with what happened (log ratio) · 3 · standardise by recent typical variation · 4 · FOCuS accumulates evidence of a lasting shift · 5 · alarm when the evidence crosses a threshold set by simulation.
