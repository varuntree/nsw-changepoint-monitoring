# Frozen scored-event set (pre-registered Thu 22 Oct 2026, before any re-run)
- Source: events_verified.csv (71 rows, sourced, 22 Oct). Scored = high-confidence *disruption* rows inside the monitoring period (1 Mar 2020 - 28 Sep 2026) that affect at least a full day of service or travel; one-off events under a day (8 Mar 2023 radio failure, 90 min) are explanations, not scored.
- Policy (fares, free travel), structural (Metro, line closures) and fault rows (publisher-flagged periods, reader-off fare-free days) are **explanations** only.
- An alarm is "near a real change" if within +-7 days of any boundary date below. An episode is "caught" if an alarm falls 0 to 7 days after one of its boundaries and moves in that boundary's direction (down for an onset, up for an end), with each alarm credited to its nearest boundary only. 18 boundaries, 15 episodes. (Scoring rule amended 23 Oct 2026, after the first scored run, because the same-weekday difference echoes each one-day fall as a rise a week later: the original rule was +-7 days with no direction check. Both rules are reported in the thesis, the original one as the pre-registered analysis; the event list itself is unchanged.)
- `announced_before`: whether the change was publicly announced before travel changed (yes/partly/no); "no" episodes carry the "before it is announced" question.
- This file is not edited after the first scored run; any change would be logged with its reason.
